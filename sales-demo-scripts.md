## Script 1 — B2B SaaS / Revenue Generation (~2 min)

**[Opening]**

Thank you for getting back to me, [First Name]. Quick walkthrough of how we
run cold outbound for teams like yours — high level — and why we're confident
we can help.

**[Process]**

First we map your TAM and lock your ICP, then build the list. The list
dictates the whole operation — without a good list, you're nowhere. We source
and enrich contacts, and tier Hot, Warm, or Nurture so the messaging matches
reality.

Then infrastructure: separate domains and mailboxes, warmed properly, on
Instantly or Smartlead, tied into your CRM and an AI reply layer — so we never
burn your main domain. And if this is LinkedIn, we'll set up your LinkedIn
accounts in the sequencer as well.

By end of week two we launch, ramp carefully, stay out of spam. Reply rate
up, bounce rate down. From week three: first responses and real buyer
conversations — a lot of our clients see that early. After week four, weekly
analytics: what's working and cost per meeting.

**[3-Month Plan]**

We ask for three months. Month one is install and first signal — results can
show in week one, but that's not the whole game. Month two is iteration:
double down on winners, kill losers, run experiments, warm extra capacity.
Month three is message-market fit — the most important idea in cold outbound:
who, what, and when that actually produces meetings. Once we have it, we put
real volume behind that campaign. More mailboxes without fit just multiplies
noise.

**[Close]**

Same system helped Linxx book opportunities and signed users, Apala Capital
run acquisition conversations at scale, and Cloudvisor engage prospects in
week one. Happy to go deep on a call if this makes sense — thank you for
watching.

tier Hot, Warm, or Nurture so coverage matches thesis and timing.

weekly analytics: actionable replies and conversations, not vanity opens.

We ask for three months. Month one is install and first signal — early
conversations can show in week one, but that's not the whole game. Month two
