# Messaging & Verbiage Bible — B2B Tech vs PE / M&A

**Purpose:** Single source of truth for how AptAI talks about the *same process* in two verticals. Use this when writing website copy, demos, Looms, sales calls, decks, or cold email so nouns match the buyer’s world.

**Live product:**

| Vertical | Demo URL | Product frame |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| **B2B Tech / SaaS** | `/` · Revenue Gen | Cold outbound engine · pipeline · meetings |
| **PE / M&A / Financial services** | `/PE` · Deal Origination | Proprietary deal origination · owner conversations · mandate-fit targets |

**Core rule:** The *spine* is identical (map market → define fit → build list → infrastructure → launch → analytics → 3-month validation → proof). The *nouns* change. Never paste GTM language onto PE, and never force PE jargon onto a SaaS buyer.

---

## 1. One process, two languages

### What is shared (say on both sides)

- We install a **done-for-you system**, not a data dump.
- **Week 0–2:** foundation + infrastructure + launch/ramp.
- **Week 3+:** first real conversations often appear early (we are proud of week-one / week-three signal).
- **Three-month commitment** is how learning compounds.
- **Message-market fit (MMF)** is the point of the circus: proven **who + what + when**.
- More domains/mailboxes **without** MMF multiplies noise (and for PE, reputation risk).
- Month 2: double down / kill / experiment + **warm extra capacity** for Month 3.
- Month 3: ~**3× volume** behind the winner; steady pipeline after the spike.
- Protect the **main domain**; warm secondary stacks (SMTP / Google / Microsoft).
- Platforms: Instantly or Smartlead; CRM sync; AI reply layer; LinkedIn sequencer when relevant.

### What must diverge

| Dimension | B2B Tech | PE / M&A |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| What we’re building | Revenue / pipeline engine | Proprietary **origination** engine |
| Who is on the list | Buyers of a product/service | Acquisition **targets** (owners, CEOs, founders) — not “customers” of the fund |
| Success event | Meeting · SQL · opportunity · closed revenue | Owner conversation · brief accepted · mgmt meeting · NDA · diligence path |
| Qualification language | ICP fit · budget · revenue · in-market | **Mandate-fit** · thesis · check size · ownership · not in competing process |
| Free-value front end | Offer / lead magnet / audit-style hook | **Approach asset** (e.g. Ownership Fit & Value-Driver Brief) |
| Activity noun | Campaign · sequence · cadence | **Sourcing cadence** · **coverage** (prefer over “campaign”) |
| Status word they respect | “Predictable pipeline” | **“Proprietary deal flow”** over broker auctions |

## 2. Master vocabulary swap

Use the **right column** for PE. Do not “translate in your head” mid-sentence on a PE call — write it native.

| # | B2B Tech / SaaS | PE / M&A / Deal Origination | Why |
|---|-----------------|----------------------------|-----|
| 1 | **GTM** / go-to-market | **Avoid entirely** for PE. Use origination / proprietary sourcing | GTM = selling a product to choosers. Targets are not buying a product. |
| 2 | **TAM** / Total Addressable Market | **Market Map** / **Target Universe** | PE already builds “market maps.” |
| 3 | **SAM / SOM** (if used) | Serviceable map / mandate-fit pool / priority queue | Keep funnel logic; drop SaaS acronyms unless they already use them. |
| 4 | **ICP** (Ideal Customer Profile) | **Investment Criteria** · **Acquisition Criteria** · *Ideal Company Profile* only if redefined | No “customers” pre-deal. Criteria = EBITDA/revenue band, sector, geo, ownership. |
| 5 | Customer / prospect / lead | **Target** · **owner** · **founder** · **decision-maker** · company | Person on list is not a “lead” in the SDR sense for PE. |
| 6 | Lead list | **Target list** | Same object, different status. |
| 7 | Lead gen / outbound lead gen | **Proprietary sourcing** · **off-market origination** | PE buyers are allergic to “lead gen agency” framing. |
| 8 | Cold outbound / cold email | OK as *channel*, but lead with **proprietary outreach / sourcing** | Cold email is the medium; proprietary is the product. |
| 9 | Campaign | **Sourcing cadence** · **coverage** · wave | “Campaign” reads marketing-flavored. |
| 10 | Sequence / multi-touch campaign | Multi-touch **approach** · cadence | Same. |
| 11 | Pipeline (sales) | **Deal pipeline** · **origination pipeline** | Pipeline is fine if qualified as *deal* / *origination*. |
| 12 | Hot lead | **Actionable** · mandate-fit opportunity · warm owner path | |
| 13 | Meeting booked | **Owner conversation** · call · management meeting · NDA path | |
| 14 | Cost per meeting | **Cost per conversation** | |
| 15 | Open rate obsession | Deprioritize; prefer **actionable reply**, conversations | Especially PE — vanity opens kill trust. |
| 16 | Cold traffic offer / lead magnet | **Approach asset** | Same mechanic (value before ask), different artifact. |
| 17 | CTA: “open to 15 min?” | CTA: **permission to send the brief** (“Would it be useful if I sent that over?”) then call | Calendar-first underperforms for owners. |
| 18 | Buyer persona (VP Sales, CMO) | **Your-side** personas: operator-led acquirer · platform origination lead | Plus **target-side**: owner/founder. |
| 19 | In-market signal (hiring SDRs, funding) | Succession / transition / carve-out / thesis-fit signals | |
| 20 | Launch campaigns | **Activate sourcing** | Live demo nav. |
| 21 | Outbound analytics | **Origination analytics** | Live demo nav. |
| 22 | Infrastructure | **Origination infra** (or infrastructure is OK if context is clear) | |
| 23 | Reply rate (generic) | Reply rate + **actionable** rate (owner / advisor paths) | |
| 24 | SDR / AE handoff | Deal-team handoff · opportunity owner | |
| 25 | Suppress customers / closed-won | Suppress **active processes**, DNC, competing banker processes | |

### Shared words that work on both sides (keep)

- List (with the right qualifier: lead list vs target list)
- Enrich · score · tier (Hot / Warm / Nurture)
- Domains · mailboxes · warmup · deliverability · bounce rate
- Instantly · Smartlead · CRM · AI reply agent · LinkedIn sequencer
- Pre-flight / pre-launch checklist · ramp
- Message-market fit · experiment · double down · kill losers
- Three-month validation sprint

## 5. Phase-by-phase messaging (what to say)

### Phase 01 — Map the market

**B2B Tech — say:**
- “First we identify your **TAM** — total addressable market — then narrow to the ICP pool we can actually reach.”
- “Without a good list, we’re nowhere. **The list is the messaging itself.**”
- Numbers story: big TAM → SAM → obtainable → best-fit ICP pool.

**PE — say:**
- “First we build your **Market Map** — the full **universe of companies** in your thesis sectors that could be **acquisition candidates**.”
- “Without a good **target list**, we’re nowhere. **The list is the origination strategy itself.**”
- Numbers story: target universe → serviceable map → **mandate-fit** → **priority queue** (not already in a competing process).
- Edge line: “That’s the edge **proprietary origination** has over **broker-run auctions** — we pre-qualify before we reach out.”

**Do not say on PE:** “TAM mapping,” “serviceable obtainable market for your product,” “customers in category.”

---

### Phase 02 — Fit definition & list

**B2B Tech — say:**
- **ICP** = who buys *your* product: firmographics, budget/revenue band, stack, triggers (funding, hiring SDRs, new CMO).
- Personas: Revenue/GTM lead, Marketing operator, Agency operator (example set).
- “We score accounts; score below threshold is out.”
- Source → enrich (waterfall) → AI score/dedupe → **campaign-ready**.
- Tiers: Hot / Warm / Nurture by **in-market** signals.
- Output: leads with email/LinkedIn, triple-verified.

**PE — say:**
- **Investment criteria** = who fits *your thesis*: check size, EBITDA/revenue, sector, geography, ownership type, not in process.
- “Ideal **company** profile,” not ideal **customer** profile — or just say criteria.
- Your-side personas:
  - **Operator-led acquirer** (search / partner-equity): successor-operator, not “just PE.”
  - **Platform origination lead** (permanent-hold): channel must stop leaking; not more raw leads.
- Source → enrich owners → score/suppress → **sourcing-ready**.
- Tiers by succession/transition/thesis signals.
- Output: owners/founders/CEOs, not “SDRs at target accounts.”

**Do not say on PE:** “ideal customer,” “budget-qualified buyers,” “MQLs,” “lead gen list.”

---

### Phase 03 — Infrastructure

**Both — say:**
- Separate domains (not main domain); mailboxes; 2-week warmup; SMTP / Google / Microsoft stacks.
- Instantly or Smartlead; CRM sync; AI reply agent.
- “If this is LinkedIn, we set up LinkedIn accounts in the sequencer as well.”
- Live cost/volume sliders are real Operator economics (optional verbal deep-dive on call only).

**B2B Tech flavor:**
- “Outbound infrastructure so cold traffic doesn’t land in spam.”
- Goal: high-volume safe sending into **buyer** inboxes.

**PE flavor:**
- “Origination infrastructure so **proprietary outreach** doesn’t land in spam — and **we never burn the firm’s primary domain.**”
- Goal: credible, confidential outreach to **owners**; reputation is existential.

**Approach asset (PE, under AI / offer beat):**
- Name: **Ownership Fit and Value-Driver Brief**
- One-page, confidential, relevance — **not** a valuation, LOI, or acquisition decision.
- CTA: *“Would it be useful if I sent that over?”*
- Explicitly reject: indicative valuation ranges as front-end bait.

**B2B offer beat (when needed):**
- Lead magnet / audit / free value offer — still value-before-calendar, but product-shaped, not M&A-shaped.

---

### Phase 04 — Launch / activate

**B2B Tech — say:**
- “**Launch campaigns** after pre-flight.”
- Ramp daily volume; watch **reply rate up, bounce rate down**.
- Waves: T1 founder/hot send, T2 multi-touch, T3 nurture.
- Outcome: **buyer conversations / meetings**.

**PE — say:**
- “**Activate the sourcing cadence** after pre-flight.”
- Same ramp discipline; same bounce/reply care.
- Waves: T1 partner/senior touch, T2 multi-touch coverage, T3 long-hold seed.
- Outcome: **owner conversations**, brief accepts, intros — not “SQL booked.”

**Both avoid on Loom:** 12-point checklist minutiae (save for call).

---

### Phase 05 — Analytics

**B2B Tech — say:**
- “**Outbound analytics** — weekly reports, experiments, **cost per meeting**.”
- Opens can appear but don’t lead the story if the brand is plain-text / no open tracking.

**PE — say:**
- “**Origination analytics** — send health, **actionable replies**, conversations, experiments.”
- “Not vanity open rates — **real sourcing progress**.”
- **Cost per conversation.**

**Kill trust (PE-specific lines to teach against):**
- Generic “partnership” language that hides buy intent.
- Unsupported portfolio/capital claims.
- Duplicate outreach into an active process.
- Reporting sends/opens instead of conversations.

---

### Phase 06 — 3-Month Validation Sprint (shared spine, dual nouns)

| Month | Shared meaning | B2B Tech wording | PE wording |
|-------|----------------|------------------|------------|
| **1** | Install + first signal | Full engine live; first **buyer** replies/meetings; results *can* show week 1 | Full engine live; first **owner** conversations; early signal week 1 |
| **2** | Iterate + warm capacity | Subjects, openers, offers, segments; warm extra domains for scale | Sector, **buyer voice**, **approach asset**, cadence; warm extra domains |
| **3** | MMF → scale | Put ~3× volume behind winning **campaign**; steady **pipeline** | Put ~3× volume behind winning **sourcing package**; steady **proprietary pipeline** |

**Message-market fit — B2B:**
> Proven pairing of **who** (ICP segment) + **what** (offer/message) + **when** (cadence) that reliably produces **qualified meetings**.

**Message-market fit — PE:**
> Proven pairing of **who** (mandate-fit targets) + **what** (buyer voice + approach asset) + **when** (cadence) that reliably produces **confidential owner conversations**.

**Shared closer:**
> “That’s the point of the entire circus. More mailboxes without message-market fit just multiplies noise.”  
> PE add: “…and reputation risk you cannot afford.”

### Phase 07 — Case Studies (proof language)

| Vertical | Named proof | Metrics language |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **B2B** | **Linxx** · **Cloudvisor** | Sent, reply rate, opportunities; LinkedIn accepts / interested (Cloudvisor) |
| **PE** | **Apala Capital** · **Permanent Corp** | Sent, reply rate, **seller / owner conversations**, week-1 opportunities |

**B2B proof lines (demo + verbal):**
- Linxx: volume → opportunities (e.g. 15.4K sent, 2.67% reply, 38 opportunities).
- Cloudvisor: week-1 email signal (e.g. 7.17% reply) + LinkedIn first two weeks (accepts, message replies, interested).

**PE proof lines:**
- Apala: **23 seller conversations** / opportunities in acquisition pipeline; successor-operator positioning over generic PE capital language.
- Permanent Corp: week-1 proprietary signal (e.g. double-digit reply, 11 opportunities) — channel installing, not “lead gen campaign.”

**Do not:**
- Put Linxx/Cloudvisor as primary PE proof, or Apala/Permanent as primary SaaS proof (cross-link demos if needed, don’t mash verticals).
- Use “Apollo” — firm is **Apala Capital**.
- On PE public UI, avoid inventing unapproved claims beyond agreed metrics.

## 9. Two layers of “persona” (do not mix)

This is the most common PE messaging mistake.

| Layer | Who | Role in the motion |
|-------|-----|--------------------|
| **A. AptAI client** | PE firm, search fund, permanent-hold acquirer, M&A / origination lead | **Buys AptAI.** Demo is *for them*. Case studies name them (Apala, Permanent Corp). |
| **B. Outreach persona / list** | **Business owners** of mandate-fit operating companies (“portco-style” sellers) | **People we email/LinkedIn on the client’s behalf.** Seller leads who may sell, merge, or partner. |

### B2B Tech (for comparison)
- Client on the call: VP Sales / Demand Gen / agency founder → buys **pipeline**.
- List personas: their ideal *customers* (e.g. CRO, CMO at target accounts).

### PE — what the demo must show
- **Investment criteria** = the **fund’s** filters (check size, sector, ownership, not in process).
- **Personas on the “persona” tab** = **sellers / owners**, not PE professionals.
  - Founder-owner (succession / fatigue)
  - Family / multi-gen owner (liquidity / estate)
  - Operator-seller (capacity ceiling / partner exit)
- Messaging, approach assets, and A/B tests are written **to owners**, not to “Head of Origination.”
- We still **sell the engagement to PE/M&A** (“we get you proprietary seller conversations”).

### Say this
> “You [PE firm] are our client. The list is **owners of companies that fit your mandate** — seller leads, not other investors.”

### Not this
> Persona cards titled “Platform Origination Lead” / “Operator-Led Acquirer” as if *they* are who cold outbound is written to.

## 10. Implementation checklist (website / demo / copy)

When adding or editing copy in this repo:

1. **Pick vertical first** — `index.html` (B2B) or `pe/index.html` (PE). Never one blob for both.
2. Run the **vocabulary swap table** (§2) on every heading and CTA.
3. Match **nav labels** exactly to §4 so Loom click-alongs stay true.
4. Keep **MMF + 3-month** structure; only swap outcome nouns (§5 Phase 06).
5. Proof: B2B = Linxx/Cloudvisor; PE = Apala/Permanent Corp (§5 Phase 07).
6. Ban list for PE pages: GTM pipeline, TAM as primary label, “customers,” lead gen agency, calendar-first as only CTA, valuation bait.
7. Ban list for B2B pages: forcing “market map” / “mandate-fit” / “origination” if it confuses a SaaS buyer (optional color is OK; don’t replace their native words).
8. Scripts: [`sales-demo-scripts.md`](sales-demo-scripts.md) — keep ≤ ~2 min for Loom; deep detail on the live call.
9. If Operator SOPs change (infra costs, caps), update ESP copy in **both** demos with the same numbers, different surrounding nouns.
